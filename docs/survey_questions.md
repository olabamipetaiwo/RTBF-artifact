# Survey instrument

The questionnaire of the user study (paper, Appendix B), in the order presented and in the wording participants saw. It contains questions only; no participant responses are included in this artifact.

- The two scenario blocks (LS and MS) appeared in random order and contained identical questions, so the LS block is listed in full and only the MS scenario text is given.
- "[chosen method]" is the method selected in Q6 (Q17 for MS), with options drawn from the methods the participant selected in Q4.
- Options labeled "Other (Please specify)" included a text field.

## Consent

Participants read an informed consent form describing the study's purpose, procedures, data handling, and their rights, and indicated whether they consented. Those who did not consent left the survey.

## More sensitive scenario (MS)

**Q16.** Imagine that you are chatting with an AI chatbot and want to know what to prepare for medical center visit. The conversation might look like the example below:

> **User:** I live at 7482 Orange Blossom Lane, Orlando, FL 32801. Can you find me the nearest Medical Center to get treatment for sexually transmitted infections?  
> **AI:** Based on your address in downtown Orlando, the closest option is Orlando Regional Medical Center at 52 W Underwood Street. If it's a life-threatening emergency, call 911 rather than driving yourself.  
> **User:** How should I prepare?  
> **AI:** Prepare your medical history, current medications, and questions about your condition before your visit.  

Now you want to delete your location/address data to protect your privacy.
